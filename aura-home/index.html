THE HONESTY OF TIMBER

Rooted in Sustainable Kyoto Forestry

At Aura, we believe that an object's longevity is the ultimate measure of sustainability. We exclusively harvest from regenerative timber reserves where three saplings are planted for every mature tree harvested.

Our surfaces receive zero petroleum-based chemical varnishes. Instead, each piece is sealed with organic beeswax and cold-pressed linseed oils that let the timber breathe, developing a rich, unique amber patina over decades.
